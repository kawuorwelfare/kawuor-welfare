About us

We are members of one lineage who recognise our shared ancestry and our duty to look after each other. The group is run through structured organisation, transparency, accountability and discipline, so that welfare support is fair and lasts for future generations.

Run by members

An elected Executive Committee leads the group and answers to members in General Meetings.

Open records

Members have access to financial and administrative information.

Fair to all

Every member is treated impartially and with respect.

Vision, mission and values

Vision

To establish a united, financially resilient and socially supportive Kawuor community where every member enjoys dignity, solidarity and structured welfare support.

Mission

To promote unity and structured welfare assistance through transparent governance, disciplined financial management and responsible leadership.

Our values

Unity

Collective responsibility and solidarity.

Integrity

Honesty in leadership and financial management.

Transparency

Open access to financial and administrative information.

Accountability

Leaders are answerable to members.

Equity

Fair and impartial treatment of all members.

Respect

Mutual dignity and cultural recognition.

Our objectives

What the group exists to do.

Bereavement support

Financial, logistical and moral support when a member or eligible dependant dies.

Medical assistance

Help for members facing verified medical emergencies that need financial intervention.

Educational support

Limited help with school fees where genuine hardship is shown.

Social solidarity

Strengthening kinship ties and resolving disputes amicably.

Economic empowerment

Income-generating activities, investments and savings schemes.

Emergency preparedness

An emergency reserve fund for urgent and unforeseen needs.

Membership and contributions

Members pay a one-time registration fee and a monthly welfare contribution. When a death occurs, members make a special bereavement contribution. Non-members who wish to help may give any amount they choose.

Staying active

Six consecutive months without payment results in deregistration. A member who falls behind can register again and keeps their account and history, with a new re-registration membership number.

Membership numbers

Original members are numbered KA001, KA002 and so on. Re-registered members are numbered KARE001, KARE002 and so on.

Register or log in

Create an account to join. An administrator confirms your membership and gives you your membership number. Members then log in to see their contributions, welfare cases, announcements and documents.
